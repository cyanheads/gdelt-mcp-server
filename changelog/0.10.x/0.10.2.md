# 0.10.2 — 2026-11-24

## Changed

- **The `query` descriptions on the other tools say boolean OR goes in parentheses**, and `effectiveQuery` says it can be the grouped query.
- **`gdelt-guide` skill 1.5** covers OR in parentheses and the mirror's slot-by-slot continuation under 60 minutes.

## Fixed

- **A query that is only terms, or only source filters, joined by OR in any case runs as one group** on all nine query tools: `climate or energy` runs as `(climate OR energy)`, `(flu OR cold) OR rsv` as `(flu OR cold OR rsv)`. `effectiveQuery` and the notice show the group ([#101](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/101)).
- **OR outside parentheses beside other elements fails `invalid_query` before any request**: `climate OR energy sourcelang:english`, a source filter joined to a term (`prix or sourcelang:french`), a phrase in typographic quotes (`“life or death”`), and `OR climate`. A lowercase `or` first or last in the query stays a word ([#101](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/101)).
- **The mirror reads `or` in any case between the elements inside parentheses**, so a mirror answer no longer treats `climate or energy` as three required words; it is grouped or refused ([#101](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/101)).
- **`gdelt_search_articles` at the 250 ceiling for a window under 60 minutes returns `continuationWindows`, one per 15-minute slot the mirror holds**; a capped single-slot answer says to narrow the query. Where no mirror can answer, the notice is unchanged ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/100)).
- **A capped DOC answer's notice names the part of the window the mirror holds** when it holds only part ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/100)).
- **A mirror answer states how many articles it matched**, and exactly 250 matches is not reported as capped ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/100)).
- **A TV rejection of a `station:`, `network:`, `show:`, or `market:` selector inside an OR group with a term carries a hint** to group selectors alone or leave them outside the group.
