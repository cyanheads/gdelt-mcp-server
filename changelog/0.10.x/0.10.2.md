# 0.10.2 — 2026-11-24

## Changed

- **The `query` descriptions of `gdelt_get_coverage_timeline`, `gdelt_get_coverage_breakdown`, `gdelt_get_tone_distribution`, `gdelt_search_tv`, and `gdelt_get_tv_breakdown` say boolean OR goes in parentheses.** On all nine query tools, `effectiveQuery` and `notice` cover the grouped query and `invalid_query` the refused OR.
- **`gdelt-guide` skill 1.5** covers OR in parentheses and the mirror's slot-by-slot continuation under 60 minutes.

## Fixed

- **A query that is only terms, or only source filters, joined by OR in any case runs as one group** on all nine query tools: `climate or energy` runs as `(climate OR energy)`, `(flu OR cold) OR rsv` as `(flu OR cold OR rsv)`. `effectiveQuery` and the notice show the group ([#101](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/101)).
- **Any other OR outside parentheses fails `invalid_query` before any request**: one beside other elements (`climate OR energy sourcelang:english`), one joining a source filter to a term (`prix or sourcelang:french`), one inside a phrase in typographic quotes (`“life or death”`), and one with nothing to join (`OR climate`). A lowercase `or` first or last in the query stays a word ([#101](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/101)).
- **A mirror answer to `climate or energy` matches either word**: it required all three, `or` included, and the mirror is now asked for the grouped query ([#101](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/101)).
- **The mirror reads `or` in any case between elements inside parentheses**, so it answers `(climate or energy)`, which it refused ([#101](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/101)).
- **`gdelt_search_articles` at the 250 ceiling for a window under 60 minutes returns `continuationWindows`, one per 15-minute slot the mirror holds**; a capped mirror answer of a single slot says to narrow the query. Where no mirror can answer, the notice is unchanged ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/100)).
- **When such a window has slots before the mirror's oldest or after its newest, a capped DOC answer's notice names the part the mirror holds** ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/100)).
- **A capped mirror answer states how many articles the mirror matched**, and an answer holding every match, such as exactly 250, is not reported as capped ([#100](https://github.com/cyanheads/gdelt-mcp-server/issues/100)).
- **A TV rejection of a `station:`, `network:`, `show:`, or `market:` selector inside an OR group with a term carries a hint** to group selectors alone or leave them outside the group.
